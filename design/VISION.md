# Propane — vision

What Propane is and must stay, learned from building and playing it. These outlast any feature, mode, platform or
version.

## The point

Propane is about shooting propane tanks in a toy suburb and enjoying what happens next. Nothing in it is hard to copy;
what makes it worth playing is how it feels. Every change answers to two questions: is it more satisfying to play, and
does it make you want another round?

## The tank is the hero

- **Two shots.** The first punctures a tank: it vents fire from the hole, forever, and its own jet drives it around,
  skidding, spinning and tumbling into things. The second shot blows it up.
- **Blasts chain.** A tank close to a blast takes it as a shot, so a pile goes up one after another. Tanks come in
  piles so that they chain.
- **It is the one real thing.** The tank is realistic in a stylized world, so it always reads as the prize and the
  target.

## Everything moves, including you

- Loose things have weight and fly when a blast reaches them: cars, bins, fence panels, tanks and players. Houses,
  trees and the ground hold.
- Arcade, not simulation. Physics serves spectacle and readable cause and effect, never realism for its own sake.
- The mess stays. Debris and scattered props build up over a round, a record of what happened.
- A blast throws you as a ragdoll. Being thrown is the price of standing too close: funny and costly, never
  frustrating.

## Feel

- **Instant.** The player's own actions take effect on their screen at once. In multiplayer, small disagreements
  between screens are the accepted price of that.
- **Moving and shooting feel natural.** The character moves with weight and polish, and the rifle sits in their hands
  like a real one.
- **Tuned by playing.** Every feel and effect value is a live dial; the right value is found in play, not in a spec.
- **Short rounds with a payoff.** A cleared suburb gives way to a new one; a match lasts a few minutes. Another round
  should always be tempting.

## Look

- **A white void.** A sterile, high-tech construct, like a VR space. The suburb sits on it as a diorama on a slab
  with crisp edges, and nothing exists beyond the play area.
- **Golden hour.** A low orange sun, long shadows and cool light in the shade.
- **A toy suburb.** Clean and stylized, built from reused parts in varied colors, at real-world scale.
- **The construct builds itself.** Worlds and objects appear and leave by scanning, dissolving and materializing.
- **Generous explosions.** Fireball, shockwave, flash, shake, debris and scorch: every blast is worth watching.

## Reach

- Built to be picked up by many people: free or cheap, on PC first (Windows, macOS and Linux) and consoles later.
  Anything sold is cosmetic; nobody buys an advantage.
